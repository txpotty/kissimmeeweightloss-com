    Who This Protocol Is For

    Dosing and Schedule in Ocoee

    The schedule depends on the drug and the parasite. For pinworm, a single dose of mebendazole 100 mg or albendazole 400 mg is typical, with a second dose two weeks later for newly hatched worms. Ivermectin depends on your body weight. It is usually given as one dose or a short course of one to two days. Your provider writes out exact dates for every dose.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Most courses take one to three days, sometimes with a repeat dose two weeks later. Some people feel a little queasy or tired for a day after the pills. With pinworm, the itching usually eases within a few days, and the remaining irritation can take another week to settle. Week two is the repeat dose. A check or a follow-up test may be suggested about a month after treatment.

    Side Effects and Precautions in Ocoee

    Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhea and headache are the side effects people mention most often. Ivermectin can occasionally cause rash or itching, especially as parasites die off. Because mebendazole and albendazole stay mostly in the gut at standard doses, short courses rarely cause much trouble. Pregnancy changes things. Some anti-parasitics are avoided in pregnancy, so tell your provider if there is any chance.
